## ARCHITECTURE

### Layer 1: Public-Facing News Platform
- AI-generated business/tech news with African focus
- Categories: AI, Energy, Security, Property, Automotive, Education, Business
- SEO-optimized articles published automatically 24/7
- Embedded CTAs that convert readers into quote requests
- Business Insider-style: fast-paced, data-driven, authoritative

### Layer 2: Consumer Marketplace
- Consumers request quotes for services across 5 verticals
- Multi-step forms: requirements → contact → preferences
- Phone PIN verification for lead quality
- Consumer chooses how many companies contact them (1-4)
- Verticals: Solar/Power, Security, Real Estate, Automotive, Education

### Layer 3: B2B Lead Marketplace (SolarReviews Model)
- Businesses buy qualified leads by niche and location
- Lead products: Exclusive, Duo, Quad, Unverified, Email-only
- Real-time dashboard with ROI tracking
- Auto-billing via PayFast (South Africa)
- No lock-in contracts — businesses control budgets daily

### Layer 4: AI Agent Orchestration
- ContentAI: Writes and publishes news articles autonomously
- LeadAI: Scrapes, scores, and routes leads
- AdAI: Manages Google Ads campaigns for clients
- MatchAI: Matches consumers to optimal business providers
- SupportAI: Handles customer inquiries
- AuditAI: Generates free lead gap analyses for businesses
- ComplianceAI: Monitors POPIA, CPA, and advertising compliance

### Layer 5: Revenue Engine
- B2B lead sales (primary revenue driver)
- SaaS subscriptions (LeadAI dashboard for businesses)
- Google AdSense on news content
- Google Ads campaign management fees (AdAI product)
- API licensing for enterprise clients
- Featured business listings

## TECH STACK

- **Frontend:** Next.js 14 (App Router, TypeScript), Tailwind CSS, Shadcn UI, Framer Motion
- **Backend:** Next.js API routes + Python microservices for scrapers/agents
- **Database:** Supabase (PostgreSQL) with Row Level Security
- **Auth:** Supabase Auth (email/password + OAuth)
- **Payments:** PayFast (South Africa only — no Stripe)
- **Email:** Resend
- **WhatsApp/SMS:** Twilio
- **AI Model:** owl-alpha via OpenRouter API (all AI agents use this model)
- **Hosting:** Vercel (frontend), Railway or Hetzner VPS (backend agents)
- **Monitoring:** Telegram alerts from ComplianceAI agent
